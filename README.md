# 🩺 MediExplain AI

> **Understand your medical reports in simple language.**

MediExplain AI is an AI-powered web application that helps users understand complex medical reports without requiring medical knowledge. Users can upload hospital reports, receive simplified explanations in their preferred language, understand abnormal test values, track health trends over time, and prepare better questions for their healthcare providers.

> **⚠️ Disclaimer:** MediExplain AI is an educational tool only. It does **not** diagnose diseases, prescribe treatments, or replace professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding your health.

---

## 🚀 Project Vision

Medical reports often contain technical terminology that is difficult for patients to understand. MediExplain AI aims to bridge this communication gap by converting medical jargon into easy-to-understand explanations while supporting multiple languages.
